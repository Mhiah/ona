How it works

  1. A circuit is a program. On TapeOut, circuits are built from NAND gates and stored on X Layer as NFTs. Each gate costs one NAND transistor, which is burned when the circuit is taped out.
  2. The art is the truth table. Ọnà reads the circuit's netlist from chain, evaluates it for every input, and draws one cell per input. The colours, style and palette come from a hash of the netlist, so the picture can't be changed or faked.
  3. Anyone can check it. “Verify on X Layer” asks the processor contract to evaluate sample inputs with a free eth_call and compares them with the picture.
  4. Making art buys transistors. Pieces made in the Studio are taped out on the Ọnà processor. Your wallet buys exactly as many NAND transistors as your design uses; that payment goes to the processor's creator.

TapeOut's X Layer contracts are new and not independently audited. Every transaction is shown in your wallet before you sign. Nothing here is investment advice.
