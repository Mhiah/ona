How it works

Logic you can see, check and own.

01 / 04

Write a rule. It becomes a circuit.

Pick a pattern in the Studio. Ọnà turns it into NAND gates, the one part every TapeOut circuit is built from.

↓
02 / 04

Every input gets an answer.

Ọnà runs the circuit for every row and column of the grid. Each answer colours one tile, so the logic draws the picture.

03 / 04

Anyone can check it.

Verify asks the contract on X Layer to answer sample tiles itself, for free, and compares them with the picture. No trust needed.

Mint it. It's yours.

Your wallet buys one transistor per gate and tapes the circuit out on the Ọnà processor. You own the piece, and the payment goes to the processor's creator.
