How it works

  1. A circuit is a program. On TapeOut, circuits are built from NAND gates and stored on X Layer as NFTs. Each gate uses one NAND transistor, which is burned when the circuit is taped out.
  2. The art is the answer sheet. Ọnà reads the circuit from chain, runs it for every input, and draws one cell per input. Colours, style and palette come from a fingerprint of the circuit, so the picture can't be changed or faked.
  3. Anyone can check it. “Verify on X Layer” asks the processor contract to compute sample cells for free and compares them with the picture.
  4. Making art supports the creator. Pieces made in the Studio are taped out on the Ọnà processor. Your wallet buys exactly as many transistors as your design uses, and that payment goes to the processor's creator.

TapeOut's X Layer contracts are new and not independently audited. Every transaction is shown in your wallet before you sign. Nothing here is investment advice.
