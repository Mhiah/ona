**Project Name:** Ọnà

**Project Description:**
Ọnà turns TapeOut circuits into art you can verify. A circuit on TapeOut is a small NAND-gate program on X Layer. Ọnà reads its netlist from chain, runs it for every possible input and paints the answers, one cell per input. The style and palette are derived from the netlist hash, so the same logic always produces the same picture and nobody can fake one. Anyone can press "Verify on X Layer" to have the processor contract evaluate sample cells and match them against the image.

The Studio lets anyone design a piece by writing simple bitwise rules over a grid. It compiles them to a NAND netlist, previews the artwork, and shows the exact cost. Minting buys exactly as many NAND transistors as the design uses from the Ọnà processor, then tapes the circuit out as an NFT. The gallery also renders any other TapeOut processor's circuits as art, so the whole X Layer TapeOut ecosystem becomes viewable.
